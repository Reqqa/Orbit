## 2. The problem statement

> How might we design a reliable, easy-to-learn automation product for the Orbyte
> ecosystem that removes one recurring operational burden for micro and small
> businesses, improves accuracy and consistency, and keeps people in control, so it can
> serve many SMEs?

## 3. Background

Many micro and small businesses run on manual follow-up, disconnected applications,
spreadsheets and messages. Owners spend evenings chasing supplier orders, customer
enquiries, quotations or payments, and missed deadlines and stock shortages follow.

Smaller businesses often cannot afford custom systems, or the time and cost of training
staff on complex ones. Work has to be done accurately and consistently by people with
different levels of digital confidence. A useful automation must **connect to the right
business information, respect roles and permissions, route decisions for human approval
and leave a record of actions.**

Orbyte brings these functions together in a shared business hub, so new solutions can
connect with more services and reach a wider market of SMEs without each builder
starting from scratch.

**Example ideas given (not a prescribed list):** chasing supplier orders, customer
enquiries, quotations or payments; bookings, e-invoicing readiness and licence
renewals; reducing food or material waste, or utility costs.

## 4. Who you are building for

Owners and small teams of **Malaysian micro and small businesses** that rely heavily on
WhatsApp, spreadsheets, paper and manual follow-up to manage daily operations.

## 5. What to build

A functional MVP for an automation product an SME would want to adopt. It may be a web
or mobile application, an intelligent agent, an API service or a working proof of
concept. It must demonstrate **one complete journey**:

1. **Trigger.** A business event or request starts the work.
2. **Connected data.** The automation pulls together the relevant business information.
3. **Decision logic.** Rules or AI (*only where it adds value*) decide what should happen.
4. **Human approval.** A person with the right role approves the consequential action.
5. **Visible outcome.** The action completes, the user sees the result, and it is
   recorded in an activity log.

Show how the Orbyte approach makes the solution easier to learn, integrate, govern and
reuse.

> Login, user profiles and role-based access control **may be simulated**; you do not
> need to build these platform foundations. Create your own synthetic data and interface
> or API assumptions. Your pitch should present the MVP as a potential startup product
> that could serve many SMEs through Orbyte; **you do not need access to the live
> platform.**

## 6. Must-have features

All six are required in the final product. In the preliminary round the proposal
explains how each will be delivered, and the demo shows the most important one working.

| # | Feature | What they expect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One clear SME problem** | One clearly defined and meaningful problem faced by SMEs |
| 2 | **Core automated journey** | One journey from a business input to a useful outcome, using AI only where it adds value |
| 3 | **Accuracy and ease of learning** | Improves accuracy and consistency, understood without costly formal training |
| 4 | **People stay in control** | Who may view or act on data, human approval before a consequential action, a simple activity or audit record. May be simulated; a full identity system is not required |
| 5 | **Orbyte fit and reuse** | How it fits Orbyte as a connected hub and could be adapted for many SMEs |
| 6 | **Business benefit and SDG** | The expected business benefit (time saved, errors avoided, tasks completed without training) and the SDG contribution |

## 7. Good-to-have features (optional)

Not required and **not scored separately**. They strengthen Innovation & Creativity or
Problem-Solution Fit.

- **Real-world validation** — test the problem or prototype with real SME owners or
  staff and share what you learned. *"An advantage, not a requirement."*
- **Automatic triggers** — events, schedules, business rules or an intelligent agent
  starting work automatically, with appropriate human oversight.
- **Explainable decisions** — recommendations based on connected business context,
  handing missing information or exceptions clearly to a human.
- **Reusable building block** — a connector, module or API others could build on.
- **Business model** — usage-based, transaction-based, freemium or partner model suited
  to the Orbyte ecosystem.
- **Growth path** — how the same innovation reaches different SMEs, connects with
  partner services and expands into other markets.

## 8. Rules and boundaries

- **Genuine SME need** — real problems in sales, customer service, finance,
  procurement, inventory, compliance, marketing, sustainability, logistics or market
  access.
- **Add something new** — a distinct SME business capability, **not** a reproduction of
  Orbyte's existing HR and administrative functions.
- **Fit the hub** — consistent with Orbyte as a shared business hub, not an unrelated
  standalone product.
- **Reusable** — designed for many SMEs, not one organisation's internal process.
- **Focus** — demo one clear use case; broader features are future roadmap.
- **Creative freedom** — you may invent capabilities and experiences, and need not copy
  Orbyte's current screens, but stay consistent with its platform approach.
- **Data and privacy** — **only team-created synthetic data**, no real personal,
  customer or company records. Briefly explain how privacy and Malaysia's PDPA
  principles would be handled in a real deployment.
- **Out of scope** — processing real payments, submitting statutory filings, and giving
  legal or financial advice. Preparatory workflows with synthetic data are allowed.

## 9. The marking rubric — preliminary round

> The preliminary round judges your proposed idea and gives you first proof that you can
> build it. You do not need a finished product, but you do need a **rough, working
> version of your single most important feature, shown live** and backed by your GitHub
> repository.

| Criterion | Weight | What judges look for |
|---|---|---|
| **Innovation & Creativity** | **30%** | A specific, real SME pain point rather than "SMEs are inefficient", and an original automation product with clear appeal and market potential. AI used only where it adds value. Not a re-skinned chatbot or a copy of Orbyte's existing HR and admin features. |
| **Problem-Solution Fit** | **25%** | Removes one recurring operational burden, improves accuracy and consistency, and is easy to learn. People stay in control through permissions, human approval and an activity record. Fits Orbyte as a connected hub, is reusable across many SMEs, and stays within the stated boundaries. |
| **Feasibility of Build** | **25%** | The core automated journey (trigger → data → decision → approval → outcome) running live, even roughly, and backed by the GitHub repo. Synthetic data and the team's own interface or API assumptions are in place. The team names its hardest risk and has a plan or fallback, without depending on access to the live Orbyte platform. |
| **Clarity of Proposal** | **20%** | The proposal follows problem → approach → expected outcome, and the expected outcome names a measurable benefit. Someone hearing it cold could explain it back. It follows the submission format and length limits. |

### How judging works

- A criterion's score is its weight multiplied by the band's percentage.
- **A GitHub repository link is required.** It must be public or accessible to judges,
  and **reflect what you demo.**
- **The preliminary score does not carry forward.** The final round uses a different
  rubric, shared with finalists.

### What success looks like

> A strong prototype completes one SME task more accurately and consistently with fewer
> manual steps. A new user can understand the journey without formal training, and the
> team demonstrates appropriate permissions, human approval and a simple activity
> record. It also explains the measurable benefit and how the solution could serve many
> SMEs through Orbyte.
>
> — Alpha Red Solutions
